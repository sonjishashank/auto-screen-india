Flash-Proof Kiosk Setup — Step by Step
MDM: Hexnode MDM (hexnode.com) — Indian company, ₹150/device/month annual plan, 30-day free trial. Manage ALL devices remotely from one browser dashboard.
1
Sign up Hexnode MDM
hexnode.com → Start free trial → create org account → go to dashboard. Buy annual plan (₹150/device/mo) before trial ends.
2
Factory Reset Samsung Tab A8
Settings → General Management → Reset → Factory data reset. Start completely fresh. Skip all Google account prompts.
3
Enroll via QR Code (Android Enterprise)
In Hexnode portal: Enroll Device → Android → Device Owner → Generate QR. On Tab A8 first-boot screen: tap blank area 6 times → QR enrollment mode appears → scan Hexnode QR. Device auto-enrolls as Hexnode Device Owner (DPC mode = highest control level).
4
Upload Your Ad Player APK
Hexnode portal → App Management → Enterprise Apps → Add → Upload APK. Hexnode hosts and pushes it to all enrolled devices.
5
Create Kiosk Policy
Hexnode → Policies → Create Policy → Kiosk Lockdown → Single App Kiosk → select your Ad Player APK. Enable: disable status bar, disable hardware buttons (home/back/recents), lock landscape orientation, disable notifications, disable settings access.
6
Security Hardening (Critical)
Inside policy: Disable USB debugging · Disable factory reset · Disable safe mode boot · Disable sideloading apps · Enable Knox policies (Samsung tab): restrict USB data transfer, enable tamper alert (emails you if device opened), prevent developer options access.
7
Apply Policy + Push APK
Assign policy to device group. Hexnode remotely installs your APK + applies all lockdown settings. Device reboots directly into your app — no Android UI visible ever again.
8
Physical Tamper Proofing
Get custom acrylic or metal cover plate that exposes only the screen (covers volume + power buttons). Mount using anti-theft security screws (Torx T6 — driver can't remove with normal tools). Add Kensington nano lock slot to bracket. Now driver has zero physical access to buttons.

Ad Player APK — What to Build
  • Fullscreen launcher — device boots directly to app, no Android UI ever visible
  • GPS polling every 30 seconds → checks against geofence zone database
  • Zone match → switch to that zone's ad playlist (images or 15-sec videos)
  • Offline cache — stores 50+ ads locally, runs uninterrupted 24hrs without internet
  • Watchdog service — detects crash, restarts app within 3 seconds automatically
  • Heartbeat ping — sends impression data to server every 60 seconds (for reporting)
  • Auto-sync — downloads new ads overnight or during low-data hours
  • Remote APK update — Hexnode pushes new versions silently (no driver involvement)
  • Screen brightness locked at 80% (sunlight readable, battery safe)
  • Build: Android Studio + Kotlin. Estimated dev time: 3–4 weeks by 1 developer

Driver Agreement — Key Clauses to Include
  • Device is property of RouteReach Media Pvt Ltd at all times — driver has no ownership claim
  • Driver must not remove, cover, tamper with, or modify the device in any way
  • Device must be powered on and connected during operating hours (6 AM – 10 PM daily)
  • Deliberate damage by driver = ₹5,000 deducted from incentive payments
  • Stolen device: driver must file police FIR and share copy within 48 hours
  • Monthly incentive: ₹900 paid via bank transfer by 5th of following month
  • Loyalty bonus: incentive increases to ₹1,200/month after 6 months uninterrupted service
  • RouteReach can terminate agreement with 7-day notice and immediately reclaim device
  • Driver cannot move or transfer device to any other vehicle without written permission

Get all agreements notarized (₹100–200 each). Protects your ₹15,000 hardware investment per auto.

2
LinkedIn B2B Outreach (Month 1+)
DM marketing managers at Hyderabad IT companies, hospital groups, real estate firms. Message template: "We run 50 ad screens in autos through your office area — 5,000 impressions/day. ₹6,000/month. Free 7-day trial?" Response rate 15–20% for hyperlocal offers like this.
3
Ad Agency Partnerships (Month 3+)
Boutique Hyderabad agencies each manage 10–20 clients. One agency relationship = 10+ advertisers. Offer 15–20% commission on campaign value. Start with smaller agencies (Rasta, Ideaworks, Brandlogist). National agencies after 300+ auto proof.
4
Business Associations (Month 2+)
Present at FAPCCI (Federation of AP Chambers), CII Young Indians Hyderabad, HYSEA (Software Exporters). One presentation = room of 50 business owners. Higher-value leads. Ask to be on their member newsletter too.

Self-Serve Portal (Month 2+, scales to 30% of revenue)
Small businesses sign up on routereach.in, pick zone, upload ad, pay via Razorpay — no sales call needed. Focus on making UI dead-simple. Goal: 30% of revenue by Month 6 comes from self-serve (zero marginal cost).
